The Warman Trio — 1950s Sock Hop

About this featured event

Get ready to jump, jive, and wail! The Warman Trio brings their nationally touring 1950s Family Band & Show to McAllen Mobile Park for a high-energy sock hop in Hall B, Saturday, January 30 from 7:00 to 10:00 PM. This talented father-and-daughters trio blends rockabilly flair with the Rock'n'Roll and Country hits everyone knows from the 1950s and 60s—think Elvis, Buddy Holly, Johnny Cash, and more, reimagined with crowd-engaging stage antics and non-stop dancing. All ages welcome; poodle skirts and saddle shoes optional but encouraged. Don't miss this must-see Winter Texan favorite—one of the most fun nights of the season!
