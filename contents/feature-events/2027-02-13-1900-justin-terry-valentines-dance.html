Justin Terry — Valentine's Dance

About this featured event

Celebrate the eve of Valentine's Day with a special dance featuring Justin Terry, the multi-talented singer, songwriter, and performer from Knoxville, Tennessee. Known for dynamic shows at Dollywood and Silver Dollar City and for blending country, gospel, and rock 'n' roll with heartfelt originals and beloved covers, Justin brings sparkling stage presence and crowd-pleasing sing-alongs to Hall B on Saturday, February 13 from 7:00 to 10:00 PM. Wear red if you like, grab your dance partner, and enjoy one of the season's most romantic nights at McAllen Mobile Park—all ages welcome.
